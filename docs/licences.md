### TBCL attribution

NAER's declaration requires naming the source:

> 臺灣華語文能力基準（TBCL）三等七級詞語表, 國家教育研究院 (National Academy for Educational Research), https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=41, used under NAER's open-data declaration (https://www.naer.edu.tw/PageDoc?fid=324), read 2026-11-09.

The app adds simplified forms from CC-CEDICT and leaves the levels unchanged.

### TOCFL attribution

> 華語八千詞 vocabulary data from LinZi - https://www.linzi.app/data/ (`tocfl-words.csv`, generated 2026-11-02), CC BY-SA 4.0.

- The app keeps only each word's two forms and its band, so `levels.tsv` as a whole is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- LinZi's file is used deliberately: SC-TOP reserves 華語八千詞's rights, but LinZi says its file is freely redistributable, and the owner accepted that.

## Dependencies

Every Rust and npm dependency is under a permissive licence or MPL-2.0.

- The About page lists their notices, generated by `mise licenses:generate` into the gitignored `src/pages/about/model/third-party-licenses.gen.json`. mise reruns it when a lockfile changes.
- It fails on a crate whose licence is not in `accepted` in `src-tauri/about.toml`. Check the licence before adding it.
